# Permadeath (ElRichMC) - participant skins

Edition covered: **Permadeath 1st edition** (Minecraft 1.15.2, 25 Mar 2020 - 24 May 2020, 38 players + admin account OmniRich). Permadeath 2 was announced (2020-2021) and repeatedly delayed; no evidence found that it was ever played, so no other roster exists.

All files are raw skin textures (not renders), converted losslessly to RGBA PNG, 64x64 (modern) or 64x32 (legacy format). Every participant's *current* skin (Mojang sessionserver, fetched 2026-09-27) is also in `current/<ign_lowercase>.png`.

**How the 2020 skin was verified.** Each official death card (@PermadeathSMP, tweeted within about a day of each death; contact sheets in `research/evidence/overview/death_cards/`) shows the player's 8x8 face in grayscale. Every candidate texture from the account's NameMC history was compared with its card: an exact match (largest per-pixel luminance difference below 1 level, out of 255) means that face was on the account when the player died.

Status legend: **2020+card** = NameMC dates the texture to the player's run *and* its face matches the death card exactly; **2020~+card** = same, but dated from NameMC list order plus floored ages (the exact timestamp is not shown); **card** = the face matches the death card exactly, but NameMC only logged this texture after the death (NameMC lists each texture once, so re-used or briefly worn skins get late dates). Where several of a player's textures share the same face, the one closest to the death date is used, and the body may differ; **2020 / 2020~** = dated to the run but no exact card match (or no card); **current** = today's skin, 2020 texture not found.

## Caveats

- **Shadoune666**: the file is the current skin, the only one NameMC ever logged. His 2020 texture (the card shows a black face with white eyes and a gray mouth) was not found.
- **FrigoAdri** and **Cibergun**: close but not pixel-exact card matches.
- For players who reuse one head across many skins (Mikecrack, ElRichMC, Crisgreen, KillerCreeper55, RubikYT), the card proves the face, not the body. The texture used is the face-matching one closest to the death date.

## Notes

- Methods: IGN -> UUID via api.mojang.com; current skin via sessionserver.mojang.com. History comes from NameMC (name history and skin history). Textures were fetched from `s.namemc.com/i/<id>.png` in a real browser session, because plain curl/WebFetch get a Cloudflare 403. Faces were compared against the official death cards (see the "How the 2020 skin was verified" section above).
- Accounts that are NOT the 2020 players, despite matching names today: `Killercreeper55` (no underscore), `Luh`, `LAKSHARTNIA`, the current `Lakshart` (d7bbbf3a), `alexelcapo`, `IbaiLlanos`, the current `Leyville` (268bc5af), `gona89`, and `Folagor03`.
- Also on the server but not a player: KernelFreeze's mod account `kernelCraft` (tab list [MOD]). No skin saved.
- Files are named `<ign_lowercase>.png`. `current/<ign_lowercase>.png` holds today's Mojang skin for the same UUID.
